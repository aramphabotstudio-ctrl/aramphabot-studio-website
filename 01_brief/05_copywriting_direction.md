# Copywriting Direction

## Voice

Professional, calm, poetic, architectural, precise, and trustworthy.

## Avoid

- Overclaiming
- Fake awards
- Fake client names
- Loud sales language
- Generic corporate writing

## Sample Home Hero

Headline:
Architecture of Place, Memory, and Quiet Experience.

Subheadline:
Aramphabot Studio creates architecture and interiors shaped by context, narrative, material sensitivity, and the emotional rhythm of space.

CTA:
Discuss a Project

## Sample Studio Intro

Aramphabot Studio is an architecture and interior design practice based in Bangkok, working across hospitality, residential, commercial, and experience-led spatial design. We approach each project as a dialogue between place, people, material, light, and memory.

Tell us about your site, ambition, and the experience you want to create.
