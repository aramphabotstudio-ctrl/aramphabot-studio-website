# Aramphabot Studio — Company Profile

Aramphabot Studio Co., Ltd. is a Bangkok-based architecture and interior design studio focusing on context-driven architecture, spatial storytelling, and experience-led design.

The studio works across hospitality, residential, commercial, and interior projects, including boutique hotels, resorts, pool villas, cafes, restaurants, community spaces, and private residences.

Aramphabot Studio believes architecture should create more than functional space. It should create atmosphere, memory, emotional rhythm, and a meaningful relationship between people, place, material, light, and time.

The studio’s design approach combines:
- Contextual thinking
- Clear spatial sequence
- Quiet luxury
- Material sensitivity
- Human-scale experience
- Refined proportion
- Storytelling through architecture
- Practical buildability

The studio aims to become a recognized architecture and design practice with a strong identity, high-quality presentation, and thoughtful design process.
